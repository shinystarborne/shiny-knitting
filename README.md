# Shiny Knitting

A knitting app for Windows: your patterns, projects, yarn and needles in one
place, with a row counter that follows you through the pattern.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/shinystarborne/shiny-knitting/releases/latest)**

---

## What it does

**Patterns and books.** Add your PDF and EPUB patterns and books; they are
kept safe in the app's own library. Find them again by designer, needle size,
yarn weight, difficulty, tags or a search. Every pattern gets a cover from its
own first page.

**Reading.** Open a pattern to read it, with a highlight line you move row by
row, a row counter (and counters of your own for repeats), bookmarks, notes,
highlights and drawings on the page, and pins: a part of a page kept at hand,
such as a chart or a size table. Choose your size, and its numbers are marked
all through the pattern. Point at an abbreviation (ssk, k2tog, w&t…) to see what
it means.

**Projects.** Each project has its own page and board: its pattern, yarn,
needles, gauge swatch, notes, photos and a log of how it went. The time spent
knitting is counted, and what it cost. Finished projects keep their story.

**Yarn stash.** What yarn you have, how much is left, where it is kept, and
what it is meant for. Fill a yarn in from a photo of its ball band. Deadstock
and cone yarn are counted by weight. Statistics show how much is in the stash,
what came in and went out each month, what was spent, and your goals for the
year.

**Needles and hooks.** Every size and length you own, sets included, and which
are in use on which project.

**People.** Measurements of the people you knit for, each one drawn so you know
how to take it.

**Constructors.** A top-down raglan or cardigan, a round yoke, and socks, worked
out from someone's measurements and your gauge, with the steps written out and
saved to a project as a checklist to tick off. Also: stitches for a size,
increasing evenly, a pattern at another gauge, and yarns held together.

**Charts.** Draw colourwork and lace charts, round yokes too, then see them
knitted up, read them row by row in words, print them, and knit from them on a
project's page. Lace is drawn in its stitch symbols, and every row is checked
against the stitches the row before left. The **Charts** tab keeps the charts
you knit or crochet from as pictures, to look at big or put on a project's
board.

**Also:** cheatsheets (pages kept to look things up in), masterclasses (videos
you learn from), inspiration boards, a wishlist and your favourite shops, and
Ravelry search.

For everything in detail, see the **[full guide](docs/GUIDE.md)**.

## Getting started

1. Download **Shiny Knitting_…_x64-setup.exe** from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/shinystarborne/shiny-knitting/releases/latest)
   and run it. No administrator rights are needed.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**: the installer isn't signed, which is why Windows asks.
3. Open the app and press **+ Add pattern**, or **Add folder…** to bring in a
   whole folder at once.

## Your data

Everything you add stays on your computer, in the app's own folder
(`%APPDATA%\com.shiny.knittingapp`). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use
one of the features that go online (below).

**Backups.** In **Settings → Backup**, **Make a backup…** saves your whole
library (every pattern, photo and note) to one zip file. Keep it somewhere
other than this computer. **Restore a backup…** reads one back in, after saving
what is there first.

**Online, only when you use it:**

- **Updates.** The app looks for a new version at start and every hour (you
  can switch this off in Settings).
- **Describing with a model.** Off unless you switch it on in Settings. It sends
  the first pages of a pattern (or a ball band's photo) to the language model
  you choose: one on your own computer, or an online service.
- **Ravelry, shops and the wishlist** reach those sites when you search or add
  a link.

## Updates

When a new version is out, the app says so and installs it in one click; your
library is kept. If something goes wrong after an update, **Settings → Go back
to the previous version…** installs the version before. If the app doesn't
open at all after an update, it offers to go back the next time you start it.

[What changed in each version](https://github.com/shinystarborne/shiny-knitting/releases)
